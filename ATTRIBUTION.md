# Attribution

This instrument uses MaleCNS v1.0 connectivity data from FlyEM at HHMI Janelia and collaborators, described by Berg et al. (2026).

- Project: https://male-cns.janelia.org/
- Download documentation: https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/
- neuPrint dataset: `male-cns:v1.0`
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Modifications: connections between significant bodies were filtered to weight >= 5, self-connections were excluded, weights were square-root transformed and normalized over incoming connections, and a high-weighted-degree recurrent subgraph was extracted for the display sample.

MaleCNS, FlyEM, HHMI Janelia, and the authors do not endorse this musical work. Model activity, the visual pulses, and the musical mapping are artificial and are not recorded biological activity.
